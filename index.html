Welcome Home

Thoughtful Upgrades, Versatile Living Spaces, and Exceptional Entertainment Features

Step into 1598 Lois Ann Ln and discover a home where thoughtful upgrades and flexible spaces set the stage for inspired living. From the moment you arrive, the curb appeal shines with a newer roof, modern siding, and a beautifully landscaped front walkway, inviting you inside to explore all that awaits.

The heart of the home is its luminous kitchen—refreshed with white oak cabinets, sleek countertops, and a brand-new gas range. The kitchen seamlessly opens to the family room, where modern light fixtures and recessed lighting bathe the space in a warm, inviting glow. The family room offers picturesque views of the deck and backyard, framed by lush, mature trees. On chilly winter nights, a gas-burning fireplace fills the room with cozy warmth, creating an ideal spot to unwind. For added versatility, the living room includes French doors, that can be easily converted into a private office—perfect for remote work or creative pursuits. New hardwood floors flow throughout the main level.

Venture downstairs and you'll be drawn to the finished basement, masterfully crafted for entertainment. Imagine movie nights in a cozy den, this flexible space adapts to your vision, making it the ultimate gathering spot for family and guests. The basement includes a crawl space for all your off-seasons storage needs.

Upstairs, you'll find a spacious master suite featuring a modern spa-like bathroom with dual vanities and a soaking tub — providing a private retreat with plenty of room to relax. The three additional bedrooms and a full bathroom, located next to the convenient second-floor laundry, complete the perfect family home.

Enjoy summer evenings on the expansive deck, surrounded by the privacy fence, and appreciate the peace of mind from newer windows, a whole home humidifier, and up-to-date safety features. Every detail speaks to enduring quality and comfort.

1598 Lois Ann Ln is ready for your story—offering a blend of craftsmanship, convenience, and possibility in a prime Naperville location. Neighborhood amenities include racquet club and pool (membership required), several playgrounds, and proximity to McDowell Forest Preserve. Minutes to I-88, Route 59, Metra, and downtown Naperville's dining, shopping, parks, and trails. Fresh paint throughout the entire home, nothing to do but move in.
